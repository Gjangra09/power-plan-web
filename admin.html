Who can view

Viewers see the Summary page with their own Google account. Admins can also use this page.

Alerts

A Telegram alert is sent when no new logger reading has arrived for the time below, then a reminder at the interval below until data is back, and one message when it is. Raise the numbers if the alerts are too many; lower them to hear sooner.

Data corrections

Fix bad logger data without editing it: ignore readings in a time range, or add units the logger missed in a period (or subtract units it counted wrongly). Added units are spread evenly over the period and count in the totals from then on. Raw data is never changed; turning a correction off brings back the exact previous numbers. Every change is in the Activity log with its effect.

Activity

Who signed in, what they opened, every admin change, and blocked attempts. Newest first.
